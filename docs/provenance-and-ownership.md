# Data provenance and ownership

[← Guide home](../README.md#documentation) · Document 3 of 10

Clear provenance and ownership help teams understand where a dataset came from, how it is maintained, and who is responsible for its accuracy.

## Document the data source

Each dataset should clearly identify its origin.

Internal sources may include company-owned databases, APIs, or telemetry logs. External sources may include vendor APIs or public datasets.

For each source, document:

- Source name and access link
- Data owner or maintainer
- Update frequency
- Licensing or usage restrictions
- Extraction method

## Assign dataset ownership

Every dataset should have clearly defined ownership so teams know who is responsible for maintaining and validating it.

Document:

- Primary owner
- Secondary reviewers
- Contact point for questions or clarification

## Why provenance and ownership matter

Clear provenance and ownership can help teams:

- Reduce confusion during handoffs
- Support audits and compliance reviews
- Trace data issues back to their source
- Improve reproducibility
- Establish accountability for ongoing maintenance
